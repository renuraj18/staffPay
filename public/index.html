    Salary rules

    How attendance turns into salary. Changes save automatically and recalculate the current sheet straight away.

    Rules for

    Base pay
    Working days
    Attendance
    Overtime incentive
    Each employee's own hourly rate: daily rate ÷ shift hours.
    Late arrival & early leave
    Missing clock in or clock out
    A day with only one punch is deducted like an absence.

    Default monthly salary applies to staff with no salary entered. Late arrival is measured from each employee's shift login time and early leave from its logout time (both after the shift's grace minutes); hourly rate = daily rate ÷ shift hours.
